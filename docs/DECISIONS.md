| D16 | **Concordance label taxonomy: the label says what kind of text, the date says how old** | 2026-07-25. The five labels are `explicitly in scripture` (a Purana or canonical text), `later devotional literature` (a named work outside the Puranic canon: Jayadeva, Rupa Goswami, Boru Chandidas), `taught within a tradition`, `folk legend`, `modern retelling`. The Brahma Vaivarta Purana is labelled scripture even though its extant recension is 15th to 16th century, because it is a Mahapurana and `approxDate` already tells the reader it is late. The alternative, labelling it `later devotional literature`, was rejected because it would put a Purana and a sixteenth-century sampradaya handbook under one label, and the single most valuable finding on the husband page is exactly that **Rayana is in a Purana and Abhimanyu is not**. Enforced by the velite enum, so a mislabelled claim fails the build. |

### T1 — Krishna AI / Krishna GPT demand research — DONE; decision = HOLD (D13)

**Status update 2026-07-24:** research findings below stand, but the founder decided to **HOLD** the
Krishna AI build (not Phase 2 yet) — revisit after the content launch. The recommendation text is kept for

Real API data (Keywords Everywhere volume + Ahrefs KD + SerpApi India SERPs; keys never printed). Full
report: `research/09-krishna-ai-demand.md`. Headlines:

- **"krishna ai" = 2,900/mo global and 2,900/mo India, KD 17** — generic product intent, nobody owns it.
  This is the real prize and an exact match to the "Ask Krishna AI" hero + the domain.
- Non-branded "Krishna AI" product cluster ≈ **5,800/mo**, low difficulty (KD 0-28). Demand is
  **overwhelmingly India** (aligns with the EN+HI decision, D10).
- **"gita gpt" (12,100/9,900)** is branded to incumbents (gitagpt.org, bhagavadgita.com/gitagpt, ranked
  apps). Hard to displace, low upside. Do not chase it.
- **"radha krishna ai" / "krishna ai image"** are **image intent**, not chatbot — already served by our
  image engine. Do not count as product demand.
- Competitors for "krishna ai" are individually weak (krishnaai.org, krsnagpt.com, krishn.ai, GPT-store
  shells). Winnable.

**Recommendation (superseded by HOLD):** GO (lean) — build a net-new **Radha-Krishna devotional AI** (not
another Gita-verse Q&A bot), anchored on the "krishna ai" head term, EN+HI, leveraging the image library.
**Founder decision: HOLD** (D13) — not built in Phase 2; revisit after the content launch. Until then the

## Q12. Do we publish a "How did Radha die?" page? (2026-07-25)

**No.** Written, then deleted before launch on the founder's call. The framing sits badly for a
devotional site: the traditions that develop Radharani most fully treat her as eternal, so a
"death" page misrepresents her even when the body says the scriptures never narrate one. The
demand is real, and research/07 had it as a target, but we are not chasing it.

## Q13. Do we build "Who is Radha's husband?" and questions of that shape? (2026-07-25)

**No, and this generalises.** Founder direction: the site does not build pages on contested,
gossip-adjacent questions, however much they are searched. Named at the time: who Radha's husband was,
how Radha died, Radha versus Rukmini as a rivalry, and anything whose natural form is a controversy or
a scandal.

The husband research was done and was good: fifteen sourced claims establishing that Rayana,
Abhimanyu and Ayan come from three different literatures centuries apart, and that the one Purana
naming a husband voids the marriage in the same chapter. It is archived at
`docs/research/retired/husband-claims-retired.yaml`, published nowhere, and the concordance `topic`
enum no longer accepts it, so a claim of that shape fails the build.

The test that replaces search volume: **does a reader finish this page with more love, or only with
more argument?** Where a genuine question has a contested answer, the scripture table can still show
the schools side by side inside a page whose purpose is understanding.

Do not recreate it. If the underlying query is ever worth serving, do it as part of who-is-radha
rather than as a page about her dying.
