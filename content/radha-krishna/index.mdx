Radha and Krishna are worshipped together as the union of the soul and God, the ideal of pure love in the Bhakti tradition. Krishna is the beloved Lord of Vrindavan, and Radha is his foremost devotee and eternal companion. Across the stories, prayers, festivals, and temples gathered here, their love is celebrated as the highest expression of devotion. Explore who they are, their timeless love story, and the many ways devotees honour them.
